applications.applicant_id → applicants(id) required the most careful reasoning because deleting an applicant should not automatically erase their hiring history. RESTRICT protects applications and their historical records from accidental deletion.
A developer might add ON DELETE CASCADE to applications.applicant_id to make account deletion easier. If the applicant is deleted, all their applications would automatically be deleted, causing loss of valuable hiring history.
